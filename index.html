    Recycling procedure

    1. Prepare the device

      Back up your files, sign out of accounts, and erase personal data where possible. Remove SIM and memory cards.

    2. Keep items safe

      Keep devices dry and intact. Do not open electronics or remove built-in batteries. Follow the recycler’s instructions for loose batteries.

    3. Arrange the handover

      Submit a pickup or drop-off request. Confirm that the location accepts your items before you travel.

    4. Use authorised processing

      Items should be assessed for reuse or repair, then processed by an appropriate recycler that recovers materials and manages residues.

    Battery safety. If a battery is swollen, hot, leaking or damaged, do not handle or transport it yourself. Contact the collection provider for instructions. Local regulations and recycler requirements take precedence.
