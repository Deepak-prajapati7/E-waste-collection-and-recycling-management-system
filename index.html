Electronics and recycling shops near you

Search an Indian city or postcode, or use your device location to find nearby electronics shops and mapped recycling points.

Listings are sourced from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete. Electronics shops and scrap yards are potential options, not confirmed e-waste recyclers. Call ahead to confirm acceptance, item requirements, and opening hours. Your city or postcode, or your coordinates when you choose “Use my location,” are sent to Nominatim and the Overpass API to find nearby locations. © OpenStreetMap contributors.
